# Ether v0.3.6

Highlights:

- **Decade channels on every install:** CHROMA 80, RIOT 90, ORBIT 00 and SHIFT 10 are full weekly TV channels built from your own catalogue, with rebuilt era graphics.
- **Live TV that keeps playing:** buffered recovery from provider drops and format changes, a stability setting per server and per channel, and channel visibility per app.
- **Quality profiles redesigned the Radarr/Sonarr way.** Profiles reset to the new defaults on upgrade; see Upgrading.
- **Faster starts** on Jellyfin, Emby, Silo and Stremio, and quicker debrid and add-on playback.
- **Collections start empty and grow from templates:** more than 200 templates in starter bundles, translated into all 20 console languages.
- **Sources › Statistics and Usage** show how every source is doing, plus fixes for many reported Plex, Android, Roku, Infuse and Usenet issues.

## Showcase decade channels

- **Four decade channels ship with every install:** CHROMA 80, RIOT 90, ORBIT 00 and SHIFT 10. Each has a realistic weekly schedule: dayparts, cartoons, reruns, themed prime-time nights, weekend and late movies, a Sunday marathon and short filler before midnight.
- **Built from your catalogue.** Series rotate through a channel in groups, and each one resumes at the episode it reached. Movies come from a popularity pool for the era. Schedules refresh daily without moving a programme someone is watching.
- **Hidden until they can be filled.** A channel stays off until your catalogue has enough to fill it. A decade channel you delete stays deleted.
- **Era graphics, rebuilt.** Each decade has its own broadcast package: chrome wordmarks and VCR clocks for the 80s, zine collage and VHS cues for the 90s, Y2K gel and glass for the 00s, and flat tiles and wipes for the 10s. Up next and tonight cards show a short description and the next four programmes.
- **Their own guide logos, Apple TV included** ([#391](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/391)). Apple TV loads them from a public link tied to this release; other apps get the same logo from your server.

## Live TV and IPTV

- **Commercials and bumpers from a link.** **Import video clips from a link** adds clips from YouTube and other sites to your break reels. Ether can install yt-dlp for you in one click and verifies the download, and **Update yt-dlp** keeps it current.
- **Choose which apps show each channel.** **Show in apps** on the channel drawer (and **Show in apps…** for several channels at once) controls Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Silo, IPTV apps and M3U separately. Channels are visible everywhere by default, and viewer access rules still apply.
- **Stability presets.** Choose **Fastest**, **Balanced** or **Rock solid** for the server, with a per-channel override. Larger reserves trade some distance from the live edge for more tolerance of provider interruptions.
- **Recovery without re-tuning.** Live playback rides out reconnects, missing segments and audio or video format changes, and fails over to a healthy backup source while the viewer still has buffer. Recovery may briefly show the reconnect screen.
- **Provider connections are released when playback ends**, so another channel can start under a stream limit.
- **IPTV refresh controls.** Combine a scheduled refresh with an optional refresh when viewers come back, with a minimum cooldown. A failed refresh keeps the last good lineup and guide.
- **Guide imports update only changed programmes** instead of rewriting the whole guide.
- **Guide browsing** adds a programme inspector, multi-day navigation, prime-time and late-night jumps, a channel group filter and bulk select. Thanks to @drondeseries for [#377](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/377).
- **Channel logos on Apple TV** ([#391](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/391)). With remote access on, your own virtual channels' logos reach Apple TV through a signed link that carries no token. Android guide thumbnails are authenticated, Plex Web gets working logos, and a failed logo falls back to the guide's.
- **Plex Web on Firefox** plays live channels again ([#383](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/383)).
- AI channel matching keeps going past one bad model reply, and break-return graphics stay on air when a programme has no artwork.

## Quality profiles

- **Ranked the Radarr/Sonarr way.** Releases sort by quality order, revision, custom format score, preferred protocol, indexer priority, seeders or Usenet age, and size, as in Radarr and Sonarr. Ether's streaming checks (accepted audio language, connection fit, recent playback history) sit around that order, and the fastest equal source still plays first.
- **Preferences are custom formats.** Release type, HDR and Dolby Vision, codecs, audio, Atmos, Repack/Proper, languages and resolution are built-in formats you score on each profile. TV-only formats appear only on show profiles. Thanks to @evulhotdog for [#355](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/355), which this builds on.
- **Optional alternatives at every resolution.** Turn on **Offer alternatives at every resolution** in the version-menu settings to spread alternatives across the available resolutions, up to seven versions. Auto stays first and the current behaviour stays the default.
- Movie and show profiles no longer gain duplicate copies when settings reload, and older shared profiles migrate correctly on PostgreSQL.
- Russian season and episode names no longer make a season's year look like the series premiere year.

## Collections

- **No collections on a fresh install.** Movie genres, TV networks and movie studios are now templates, in their own **Movie genres**, **TV networks** and **Movie studios** bundles. Existing installs keep every collection they have, and applying a bundle skips ones you already have.
- **More than 200 templates** in eight starter bundles, covering streaming originals, awards, seasonal picks and franchises. Bundles preview what will be created or skipped, and starter bundles are offered during setup.
- **Every template is translated** into all 20 console languages.
- **Larger MDBList lists** load in pages without duplicates, and a list that stops partway is reported instead of saved short.
- Collection pages are counted from the titles actually in the collection, fixing short pages and repeated items.

## Sources

- **Sources › Statistics** is a dashboard for all your sources: one overview chart of plays and data downloaded, failovers and failed plays, today's hourly plays and lifetime totals. Every source has a drawer with its own periods and totals, including each debrid account and every configured Usenet provider.
- **Sources › Usage** is its own tab beside it, and both keep the selected period.
- **Usenet season packs.** An episode can play straight from a season pack or a season held in a single archive, with PAR2 verification kept. Thanks to @javi11 for [#378](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/378).
- **Disc images.** ISO, Blu-ray and DVD images play their main feature, including multi-disc and multi-clip Blu-rays and discs inside encrypted archives. Thanks to @javi11 for [#393](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/393).
- **Archive and Usenet fixes:** ZIP and ZIP64 archives stream without extracting, RAR4 volume numbering, obfuscated and nested post names, UUEncode articles, faster Usenet connections across multiple server addresses, and faster yEnc decoding.
- **Anime split seasons** (cours) map to the right episodes across debrid, Usenet packs and console searches.
- **A dead debrid CDN node no longer stalls playback** ([#396](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/396)). Ether fetches a fresh link on a working node and resumes at the same position, then tries another provider.
- **Usenet speed decisions** need several slow reads and respect the grace period before replacing a stream.

## Playback speed

- **Detail pages warm up on Jellyfin, Emby and Silo**, as they already did on Plex, so Play starts sooner. Warm-ups use exactly what the device declared it can play.
- **Stremio and Nuvio warm up when you open the stream list**, before you pick.
- **Faster debrid starts.** The health check fetches the start of the file in the same request, and the first bytes go to the player as soon as they arrive.
- **Playback reads go first** when a debrid host is at its connection limit, ahead of read-ahead and prefetch.
- **Native Stremio and Nuvio players get HDR and HEVC** instead of being treated as a web browser.
- **Add-on streams are handled like Ether's own** when the add-on's link names the release, so Ether's cached checks, ranking and failover apply. A debrid-cached add-on stream gets a backup start after 2 seconds if it is slow, and a held detail page may warm the top-ranked cached add-on stream.
- Stremio add-on playback opens one stream link per Play.

## Apps and devices

- **Android phones convert Dolby and DTS to AAC when streamed**, which stops Plex for Android phones freezing after the first segment. Chromecast and onn. Google TV convert DTS and TrueHD to Dolby Digital Plus; Shield and other Android TV boxes keep passthrough. If an unknown box stalls on surround, Ether asks the viewer to play again and converts for that device from then on. Thanks to @drondeseries for [#395](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/395).
- **Plex for Roku plays 4K** when it reports a 1080p H.264 decoder alongside a 4K screen ([#394](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/394)).
- **Infuse Direct Mode** shows Ether's Home rows as their own shelves instead of folding them into Recently Added, and Watchlist titles resolve.
- **Device catalogue** has correct entries for NVIDIA Shield, Google TV and Mac.
- **Plex for LG** gets a stable Auto version ([#375](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/375)), and Plex desktop apps can download ([#365](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/365)).
- **Plex account watch sync** uses each viewer's own Plex account, including resume positions and Continue Watching removals, and respects Plex's sync setting.
- More accurate seeking on copied streams, compatible audio stays copied outside HLS ([#376](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/376)), MP3 is converted for Apple TV, and Dolby Vision profile 5 tone-maps for SDR.
- Remote addresses keep the public host and scheme you configured when you're away from home ([#381](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/381)).

## Languages and subtitles

- **Regional and script preferences** such as Latin American or European Spanish, Canadian or European French, and Simplified or Traditional Chinese.
- **Track titles fill in missing languages** when a track has no language tag.
- **Safer failover.** A normal dialogue track never silently becomes commentary or audio description, and forced subtitles are not swapped for full ones.
- **Styled subtitles and fonts for Silo.** Silo gets the original ASS styling and only the fonts it uses.

## Database and performance

- **Faster browsing on large libraries.** Totals, filters, popularity pages, Continue Watching, On Deck, people searches and play queues use smaller, batched reads.
- **A leaner catalogue.** Titles nobody has opened, played or listed live in a search-only index instead of the database, and stay searchable. On upgrade, Ether tidies an existing catalogue in the background after taking a backup; **Tidying the library** shows progress. Titles you or your viewers touched are kept.
- **Better search ranking.** A well-known title is no longer pushed down by an obscure title with the same name.
- **Database maintenance no longer blocks playback or other writes**, and daily maintenance prunes records that only grew over time.
- **SQLite to PostgreSQL** cluster setup no longer fails on a search table ([#398](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/398)). Thanks to @drondeseries.
- On Air media are stored as files instead of inside the database, and backups can leave Live TV media out.

## Owner console

- **Overview and Activity agree** on what's playing and update as soon as a stream starts or stops.
- **No false plex.direct warning** after a restart.
- **Automatic updates are on for new installs.** They install at night when nobody is watching and tell you afterwards. Existing servers keep their setting.
- **Database health** is a compact status beside **Database check**.

## Upgrading

- **Back up first:** the data directory, `config.json` and `.credentials-key`, plus your PostgreSQL database if you use one.
- **Quality profiles reset to the new defaults.** Profiles from earlier versions can't carry over to the new ranking, so every quality profile is replaced by the shipped defaults. Custom or imported profiles must be recreated or re-imported. Device rules and limits are kept.
- **The library tidy runs after the first start.** It waits for a backup, then works in the background; you can pause and resume it.
- **Collections:** existing installs keep every collection. Only fresh installs start empty.
- **Plex for Android phones** now hear converted stereo or AAC in streams instead of copied Dolby or DTS. Direct play is unchanged.
- **Clusters:** mixed versions are not supported. Stop every node, upgrade them all, then start them again.

## Known limits

- **Apple TV logos for your own channels need remote access.** Plex for Apple TV only draws logos it can fetch from the internet. Without remote access, use a public image link for the logo; the shipped decade channels work either way.
- An overloaded software encoder can still stall. This is not a guarantee for overloaded hosts or untested GPUs.
- Some of the fixes above were checked against recorded app traffic rather than on the exact devices in the reports. If something still fails, report the app and version, whether it was direct play or transcode, and attach a fresh support bundle.
