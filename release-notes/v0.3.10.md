# Ether v0.3.10

Highlights:

- **Live TV is a setup choice, and an AI builds channels with you.** Setup asks whether you want Live TV. Turning it on adds the four decade channels, and with it off Live TV costs nothing. A new channel builder interviews you (what the channel is for, commercials or none, branding) and programs it, YouTube commercials included. You approve every change before it airs.
- **Virtual channels keep the wall clock.** A channel never schedules a title without a playable source. Readiness is checked only while someone watches Live TV, and a replacement title joins mid-slot at the right point instead of starting from the beginning. On Air moments show immediately on a cold tune and reuse their rendered plates instead of re-rendering them.
- **Step aside works on every Plex surface at library scale** ([#418](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/discussions/418)). Titles you already have on your own Plex server are hidden from home rows, section pages, search and the letter jump bar, including multi-episode files such as `S04E22-E23`. Large libraries stay fast: the first check never holds up a request.
- **Pause a source without deleting it.** Debrid accounts, Usenet providers, indexers and IPTV sources each have an **Enabled** switch. A disabled source keeps its settings and comes back with one click.
- **Friends' and Home watchlists sync.** Shared friends' watchlists come in through Plex's community API, every Plex Home profile's watchlist syncs, and each viewer's page says what synced and why. Large lists sync as a background job, so a proxy timeout can't break them.
- **Watchlists say what they're waiting for.** Each title is **Not checked yet**, **No stream found yet · checked 2 h ago** or **Available**, and the page says how many titles were checked and when the next check runs. **Check availability** now checks the whole list in the background with live progress (it used to check 20 titles per click), and newly synced titles are checked first.
- **PosterFlow and custom posters** ([#445](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/445)). PosterFlow's **Asset Upload** to Plex or Jellyfin now works against Ether. You can also pick a poster folder (`{tmdb_id}.jpg` or `Title (Year)/poster.jpg`) with a folder browser. Your posters win over TMDB, poster providers and overlays, and apps refresh them without clearing their cache.

## Playback

- **Samsung TVs fall back to software encoding.** When the hardware encoder failed or stalled before the first DASH segment, the Samsung app got nothing and showed a playback error. Ether now switches to software encoding inside the same session. 10-bit HEVC and AC3 copies to Samsung TVs also use the codec strings and sample entries the TV expects.
- **A slow source recovers in seconds.** When a source can't keep up, Ether now prepares up to three lighter copies at once instead of trying one at a time. It remembers releases that turned out incomplete, so they're skipped instantly next time, and it avoids starting on a copy your debrid account has recently been too slow to deliver.
- **The right show for shared names.** A 1984 sitcom and a 2024 drama both called *Who's the Boss?* could swap releases. A release that only matches the name now loses to one whose IDs, year or episode title match, during failover too.
- **Plex Android subtitles** ([#191](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/191)). Text subtitles are served as separate sidecar tracks, as PMS does, and the stream keeps PMS's timing.
- **Availability check timeout** ([#444](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/444), thanks @javi11). Owners with slow backends can set `plex.fileCheckTimeout` in `config.json` (default `2s`).
- **Jellyfin for Roku plays** ([#421](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/421)). On Play, the Roku app asks for an item's image list. Ether had no such route and answered with plain text, so the app exited to Roku Home. Ether now serves the image list as Jellyfin does, any unknown Jellyfin or Emby route answers with Jellyfin's JSON error, and playback follows the profile the Roku posts.

## Live TV

- **USA TV Next and other relayed channels tune.** Some playlists arrive compressed without saying so, and some segment links have no file extension. Ether now decodes those playlists and names the segments, so the channels play.

## Plugins

Thanks to @FizzWhirl for a precise set of issues from building a content-kind plugin.

- **`folders.hash`** ([#447](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/447)) returns size, modification time, CRC32, MD5 and SHA-1 for a file inside an owner-granted folder. It never returns contents, keeps the same containment as `folders.stat`, and offers explicit cancellable hash jobs with progress for files over 256 MiB. Digests are cached for unchanged files.
- **Local files from a kind resolver** ([#448](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/448)): `{"pattern": "file", "path": …}`, with the same checks as a Stremio `ether.file` stream.
- **Items can say they're unavailable** ([#451](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/451)). An item upserted with `unavailable: {code, detail}` stays visible with its reason and is never played. The next upsert without it clears it.

- **Rules for plugin libraries** ([#449](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/449)). Plugin libraries get the same **Rules** tab as your own libraries, offering only the fields that kind of item actually has, with a live preview on the plugin's real items.
- **Facets on year, release date and rating** ([#450](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/450)) without declaring a duplicate field.
- **The published manifest schema covers content kinds** ([#452](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/452)), and a test keeps it in step with Ether's own validator.

- **MDBList can be switched on again.** Integrations › MDBList › **Save and enable** failed with "no such config section". Each viewer still connects their own MDBList API key on their profile.

## Owner console

- Lighter editing in the look editor and settings forms, and logs that stream live and scroll smoothly through very long histories. The console's security policy is stricter.
- Support bundles keep a short reason phrase for playback and transcode failures, so a bundle shows what failed without leaking anything identifying.

## Fixes

- **Stremio add-ons: "Try again" works and health is real.** On a plain Stremio add-on (one installed from a link, such as USA TV Next or Torrentio), **Try again** failed with "Couldn't save, something went wrong", and an add-on marked **Not answering** could never recover from it. Ether was asking the add-on for Ether's own test and health endpoints, which Stremio add-ons don't have. It now checks an add-on the Stremio way, by reading its manifest: an add-on that answers is healthy, and one that doesn't is reported as not answering. An add-on still showing **Not answering** after updating recovers with one **Try again**.
- **Dubbed releases from a regional tracker show the dub's language.** A release named only `DUAL` or `MULTI` (for example `…WEB-DL.DUAL-C76` from a Brazilian tracker) says it has two audio tracks without naming the dub. v0.3.9 stopped guessing that track's language from the viewer's own language, so the detail page showed it as *Unknown*. Ether now uses the language of the indexer the release came from. Built-in sites that declare a language use it automatically, and any indexer can be given one under **Sources › Indexers › (indexer) › More options › Release language**. A pt-BR viewer then sees *Português (Brasil)* listed and preferred, and a pt-BR quality profile ranks those releases like ones tagged `PT-BR`. A language named in the release itself always wins, and Ether still never guesses the dub from the viewer's language.
- **OpenAI models can use tools.** With OpenAI as the language model, the AI in Collections and the assistant answered "This model can't use tools" on OpenAI's newest models. Those models only accept tools through OpenAI's Responses API, and Ether now uses it for OpenAI. When a provider does refuse tools, the message now includes the provider's own reason.
