# Ether v0.3.7

Highlights:

- **High CPU after 0.3.6 fixed.** The four decade channels no longer render graphics while nobody watches them ([#409](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/409)).
- **No false alarms after an update.** Startup database work no longer fails background jobs, and health shows "checking" instead of errors while things settle ([#407](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/407)).
- **Stopped streams really stop.** A player that keeps fetching after it stopped can no longer keep a session, its ffmpeg or its Usenet reads alive ([#410](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/410)).
- **LG webOS TVs play 4K HEVC without buffering** ([#408](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/408)).
- **More Jellyfin clients work:** Litefin plays ([#404](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/404)) and Moonfin shows posters ([#405](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/405)).
- **Decade channels get era commercials and logo bugs.** CHROMA 80, RIOT 90, ORBIT 00 and SHIFT 10 ship with reels of period commercials, stored as links and fetched only when someone watches, and each channel's corner bug is now a small era logo.
- **Faster Usenet streams.** Read-ahead adapts to your provider's connections, and one slow first read no longer triggers a failover ([#385](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/385)).
- **Plex collections show all their titles** ([#411](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/411)), and **Always** rows on Home always show.
- **Home designer:** a bottom bulk-action bar like Live TV's, and the page opens many times faster.

## Performance

- **On Air graphics render only for channels someone is watching** ([#409](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/409)). In 0.3.6 the decade channels rendered their idents, break and up-next clips around the clock, which kept small servers at 200%+ CPU. Ether now renders for watched channels and for looks you preview in the console. Tuning in cold plays straight away with simple graphics until the channel's clips are ready.
- **Disabling or deleting a channel stops its rendering at once,** including its ffmpeg, instead of finishing queued work.
- **The On Air renderer is about 3.5× faster** on blur and compositing, with identical output.
- **Calmer background work:** IPTV app listings rebuild at most every 30 seconds while the catalogue is changing, and availability no longer recomputes hidden titles back to back. An unchanged hidden-title set no longer rewrites its rows every minute, which held the database for over a second on large libraries.
- **Faster Usenet streams** ([#385](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/385)). Each stream's read-ahead now uses as many of your provider's connections as its bitrate needs and shares them fairly between streams, with no new settings. On a simulated 50-connection provider, a 4K remux went from 4.5 MB/s to 15.7 MB/s.
- **No failovers from one slow read.** The first read after a start or seek no longer counts as throughput, a speed decision needs sustained evidence, and the grace period after a start, seek or failover now holds on the urgent path too.

## Updates and health

- **No more false alarms right after an update** ([#407](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/407)). Schema migrations, credential sealing and the startup checkpoint make Ether's own background jobs wait their turn instead of failing them after 10 seconds. Long index builds and the movie-year sweep no longer block other writers.
- **Health waits before calling something broken.** A catalogue or shared-user sync that hits a busy database retries within a minute and shows as syncing, not as a problem to fix. The secure-connection (plex.direct) check runs again as soon as the server is listening.
- **Sync shared users** on **Viewers** and **Apps › Plex › Remote access**, and **Run checks again** also re-syncs shared users. Connectivity logs name the exact address and port plex.tv probed.
- **Client rules that named a removed quality profile are repaired automatically.** After the 0.3.6 profile reset, such rules point to your default profile for that media and keep their other settings, so saving settings no longer fails.

## Live TV

- **Era commercials for the decade channels.** Each decade channel ships with a reel of period commercials (46 in all). They are stored as links (YouTube or archive.org) and fetched through yt-dlp only when a break is coming up on a channel someone is watching, into a 2 GB cache. A link that fails is skipped for the next clip or filler, never black or off-air. Existing installs get the reels on untouched decade channels. In your own reels, **Add as link** does the same for any clip.
- **Logo bugs.** Each decade channel's corner bug is a small era logo instead of text.
- **A cold tune-in never shows the off-air card.** While a channel's first programme is still opening, Ether holds the opening ident for up to three minutes and then joins the programme at its scheduled point.

## Playback

- **Stopped streams really stop** ([#410](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/410)). A client's stop can no longer be undone by later segment requests from a backgrounded player. Three minutes without reports from a playing Plex client ends the whole session, not just its Now Playing entry. Paused sessions still close when their 30-minute hold ends. The `encoder input producer retirement is unverified` warning is gone.
- **LG webOS and VIDAA TVs** ([#408](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/408)). When a TV's streaming player can't take the file's video codec, Ether keeps Direct Play instead of remuxing to an unplayable stream. H.264 level and transcode-target limits are respected, so a fallback transcode is 1080p, not 4K. These TVs get a single HLS variant so a quality switch can't wedge the player. The stop request after a stopped timeline no longer fails.
- **A dead TorBox CDN node no longer stalls playback.** When TorBox keeps pinning a dead node, Ether replays the link on another TorBox edge that recently served this account, never on another provider's servers. Thanks to @ogrrd for [#412](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/412).
- **Faster HDR tone mapping on Intel QSV.** Hardware decode stays on when filters run in software, filtering uses several threads, and Jellyfin ffmpeg's SIMD tone mapper runs after scaling down.

## Jellyfin and Emby clients

- **Litefin can play** ([#404](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/404)). Device profiles and play requests accept numbers or strings for every value, so one oddly typed field no longer rejects playback.
- **Moonfin shows posters** ([#405](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/405)). Image requests without a tag are served for signed-in clients as that viewer. Without any credential, title artwork is served only for titles that every viewer may see; anything restricted for even one viewer still returns not found.

## Plex

- **Collections show all their titles** ([#411](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/411)). Mixed movie-and-show collections were cut to 24 items before the shelf picked a media type, and several list views filtered after paging, so pages came back short. Lists now filter first and page over the result, and collection members that the 0.3.6 tidy moved out of the library are brought back.
- **Always rows on Home always show.** A row set to **Always** is no longer dropped for having fewer than 12 titles, for being a "new account" row, or by page caps. The designer says why a row is absent.

## Owner console

- **Home designer bulk actions** use the same floating bar along the bottom as Live TV, and selecting rows no longer shifts the page.
- **Home opens much faster.** The typical-viewer preview reuses the shared taste model, the row list no longer evaluates every collection, and the page loads its data in one parallel wave. The first preview went from about 2 seconds to under 0.2 seconds on a test library.
- **Cache Explorer: Clear without blocking** ([#402](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/402)). **Clear** drops a release's cached bytes and keeps it playable; **Clear & block** works as before.
- **Find on TMDB: why a title is missing, and Add to library.** The title inspector explains in plain words why a title isn't in your library, and **Add to library** brings in titles left out by your browse filters. Adult titles, your content rules and unreleased new movies still win.
- **Indexer statistics count real API requests,** matching your indexer's own dashboard, with cache hits and requests held back by your daily limit shown separately. Two leaks that spent background budget as if it were playback are fixed.
- **Watchlist sync** no longer logs a failure for shared friends whose Plex token Plex doesn't give the server ([#337](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/337)).
- **"Aired within" rules for TV collections** ([#406](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/406)). **Latest season premiere** and **Latest aired episode** keep returning shows with a new season in rows like "New on Netflix", and network or streaming collections ask TMDB for shows that aired in that window.

## Upgrading

- **No manual steps.** Upgrade and start Ether.
- If you disabled the decade channels to work around high CPU on 0.3.6, you can turn them back on.
- Upgrading from 0.3.5 or earlier: the [v0.3.6 upgrade notes](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/releases/tag/v0.3.6) still apply.
